# Third-Party Research & Technical Evaluation

## Investigated Projects
1. **ReXGlue SDK** (`rexglue-sdk`):
   - PowerPC dynamic and static recompilation runtime support. Big-endian memory management and XAM emulation.
2. **libadrenotools**:
   - Clean, rootless Vulkan driver injection for Qualcomm Adreno devices.
3. **Mali PanVK / ZMOD-Liberty**:
   - Workarounds for ARM Mali GPU driver quirks, clip distances, and subpass bindings.
4. **GameNetworkingSockets**:
   - Valve's reliable UDP socket abstraction with encryption and MTU management.
5. **MoltenVK & Native Metal**:
   - Direct Vulkan-to-Metal translation on iOS/macOS. Evaluated alongside pure Metal backends.
